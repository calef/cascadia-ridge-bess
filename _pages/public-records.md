title: "Public Records Requests"
---

Much of what this site documents came from public records requests under Washington's Public Records Act (RCW 42.56). This page lists each request and serves the productions we can host directly. The largest productions are being archived at the Internet Archive; links will be added here as those uploads complete. Anyone can also request the same records directly from the agency by citing the request number.

King County requests go through the [Public Records Request Center](https://kingcountywa.govqa.us/WEBAPP/_rs/). City of Snoqualmie requests go to the City Clerk.
