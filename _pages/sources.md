The claims on this site rest on primary sources: peer-reviewed research, government and agency records, regulatory filings, and recorded documents. This page collects them in one place so you can read the originals and cite them in your own SEPA comment. Each source is listed by its own name and links to the public original.

If you are writing a comment, the [how-to guide](/sepa-comments) explains how to tie a source to a specific environmental impact. Short and specific, backed by one of these, beats long and vague.

This library is expanding. More of the King County and City of Snoqualmie permitting records, and our correspondence with the agencies, are cited on the topic pages and are being added here.

## Fire and battery safety research

- **Larsson et al. (2017), "Toxic fluoride gas emissions from lithium-ion battery fires,"** *Scientific Reports* 7:10018 — [doi.org/10.1038/s41598-017-09784-z](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-017-09784-z). Measured hydrogen fluoride release across 39 battery fire tests.
- **Willstrand, Quant & Hynynen (2025), "Contaminations from Lithium-Ion Battery Fires: PFAS in Soot,"** *Fire Technology* 61:2889 — [doi.org/10.1007/s10694-025-01708-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10694-025-01708-y).
- **NIST Technical Note 2365 (2026), "Understanding the Risk of Lithium-Ion Battery Fires"** — [doi.org/10.6028/NIST.TN.2365](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.TN.2365). Multi-source data analysis from the National Institute of Standards and Technology.
- **CROSS-UK Report 1166 (2023), "Battery Energy Storage System concerns"** — [cross-safety.org](https://www.cross-safety.org/uk/safety-information/cross-safety-report/battery-energy-storage-system-concerns-1166). Expert-panel safety commentary identifying fire-plume and contaminated-runoff as the principal BESS-fire consequences.
- **American Clean Power Association (2025), "Assessment of Potential Impacts of Fires at BESS Facilities"** — [cleanpower.org](https://cleanpower.org/resources/assessment-of-potential-impacts-of-fires-at-bess-facilities/). The industry association's own fire-impacts assessment.
- **NHTSA Recall 25E007000 (2025), Samsung SDI high-voltage battery packs** — [Part 573 Safety Recall Report (PDF)](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/rcl/2025/RCLRPT-25E007-3917.PDF). Cell-separator manufacturing defect, 180,196 units.

## Documented BESS fires and their aftermath

- **Energy Safe Victoria, "Statement of Technical Findings: Victoria Big Battery" (2021)** — [energysafe.vic.gov.au (PDF)](https://www.energysafe.vic.gov.au/sites/default/files/2022-12/VBB_StatementOfFindings_FINAL_28Sep2021.pdf). The official regulator's findings on the July 2021 Victorian Big Battery fire.
- **WECC, "Moss Landing BESS Fire Report: Operations Analysis" (December 2025)** — [wecc.org (PDF)](https://www.wecc.org/sites/default/files/documents/progress_report/2025/Moss%20Landing%20BESS%20Fire%20Report%20Final%2012.22.25.pdf). The Western Electricity Coordinating Council's operations analysis of the January 2025 Moss Landing fire.
- **Aiello et al. (2025), "Coastal wetland deposition of cathode metals from the world's largest lithium-ion battery fire,"** *Scientific Reports* 15:42113 — [doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-25972-8](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-25972-8). Peer-reviewed measurement of metal deposition into wetlands after Moss Landing.
- **City of Escondido, SDG&E battery fire air-quality report (2024)** — [escondido.gov (PDF)](https://www.escondido.gov/DocumentCenter/View/6716/SDGE-Battery-Fire-Air-Quality-Report-PDF).

- **EPA Region 7, Fredericktown battery-recycling fire air-sampling FAQ and data-review memo (2025)** — [response.epa.gov (PDF)](https://response.epa.gov/sites/16725/files/CMR%20Air%20Sampling%20FAQ%20-%20March%202025b.pdf).
- **KSDK, "Nearly 3 miles of dead fish found in Fredericktown after battery plant fire" (2024)** — [ksdk.com](https://www.ksdk.com/article/news/local/fredericktown-mo-battery-plant-fire-dead-fish-kill-updates-investigation-firefighting-foam-lithium-battery-fire/63-02253d7c-de87-450d-a324-c78b5d404b88).

- **PSE 2023 Electric Progress Report, Chapter 7 (Resource Adequacy)** — [PSE (PDF)](https://www.pse.com/-/media/PDFs/IRP/2023/electric/chapters/07_EPR23_Ch7_Final.pdf).
